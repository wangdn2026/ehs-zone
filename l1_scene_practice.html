1
前台咨询 · 挂号导诊Reception & Registration
Patient 病人
Excuse me, where is the internal medicine department for fever and cold symptoms? Which floor and building is it on?
打扰一下，请问看感冒发烧的内科门诊在哪一栋楼、几楼？
Receptionist 导诊员
Internal medicine is on the 2nd floor of Building A. You can register your appointment right here first.
内科在A栋二楼，你可以先在我这里挂号。
Patient 病人
Alright, here is my medical insurance card.
好的，这是我的医保卡。
Receptionist 导诊员
Wait for your number on the screen, the doctor will call you when it's your turn.
在屏幕上等候叫号就可以，轮到你时医生会喊你的名字。
2
医生问诊 · 检查症状Doctor Consultation & Check-up
Doctor 医生
Please take a seat. What brings you in today?
请坐。你今天是哪里不舒服？
Patient 病人
I've had a bad cold for three days, with a high fever, sore throat and constant coughing.
我重感冒三天了，发高烧、喉咙疼，还一直咳嗽。
Doctor 医生
Let me check your temperature, pulse and breathing first. Have you had any chronic diseases before?
我先帮你量体温、检查脉搏和呼吸。你以前有慢性病吗？
Patient 病人
No, I've always been pretty healthy.
没有，我身体一直都挺好的。
Doctor 医生
It's just a viral cold, no serious infection. I'll prescribe some fever-reducer and cough syrup for you.
只是病毒性感冒，没有严重感染。我给你开退烧药和止咳糖浆。
3
医嘱与复查 · 早日康复Medical Advice & Follow-up
Patient 病人
Do I need to come back for a follow-up check-up?
我需要回来定期复查吗？
Doctor 医生
Only if your fever lasts more than five days. Drink plenty of water, get enough rest, and do regular exercise to boost your immunity later.
只要发烧超过五天再过来就行。平时多喝温水、好好休息，后续坚持锻炼提升免疫力。
Patient 病人
Thank you so much, doctor. I hope I can recover soon.
非常感谢医生，希望我能早日康复。
1
项目启动 · 阶段全景Project Kick-off & Four Phases
PM 项目经理
We will start a facility renovation project soon. What safety work should we arrange for contractors in advance?
我们马上要启动厂务设施改造项目，针对承包商，我们前期要做好哪些安全工作？
EHS Manager 安全经理
Contractor safety management should be included in the whole project, which is divided into four stages — and now I'll focus on an introduction to the first two stages.
承包商安全管理融入项目全程，一共分为四个阶段，本次我们只关注讲解前两个阶段。
2
资格预审 · 筛选核心Pre-Qualification & Selection
PM 项目经理
What is the core task of the first stage, Pre-Qualification & Selection?
第一阶段资格预审与筛选，核心工作是什么？
EHS Manager 安全经理
We must collect a completed Contractor Pre-qualification Form from every bidding contractor at first.
我们首先必须收取所有投标承包商填写完整的《承包商资格预审表》。
PM 项目经理
What key contents do we need to check on this pre-qualification form?
这份资格预审表，我们重点要核查哪些内容？
EHS Manager 安全经理
We check their licences, certifications, qualifications, 3-year reportable injuries, safety citations from government agencies and some items of their EHS management systems.
我们核查其执照、资质证书、从业资格、近三年可记录工伤事故、政府监管机构出具的安全违规处罚通知以及一些 EHS 管理系统方面的问题。
3
入场准备 · 培训协议Pre-Job Safety · Training & Agreement
PM 项目经理
Once we select a qualified contractor after pre-qualification, what is the next key step?
我们通过预审选定合格承包商之后，下一步关键流程是什么？
EHS Manager 安全经理
All contractor staff must finish our designated online orientation training and their specific trainings.
所有承包商人员必须完成我们指定的线上入职安全培训，还有他们自己的专项培训。
PM 项目经理
Great, training is very important — anything else?
很好，培训十分重要，还有其他事项吗？
EHS Manager 安全经理
Sure, they need to conduct hazard identification and risk assessment in advance for the contractor activities and submit their safety plan.
当然，他们事先需针对承包商作业开展危险源辨识与风险评估，并提交安全方案。
PM 项目经理
Does the second stage require them to sign a safety agreement?
第二阶段作业前安全准备，要求签订安全协议？
EHS Manager 安全经理
Absolutely, we shall sign a formal Safety Agreement with them prior to the start of work in order to comply with the Work Safety Law.
一定的，为了符合安全生产法的要求，在开工前和对方签订正式安全生产管理协议。
